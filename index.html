Our mission

Inspiration Is Everywhere and Root to Rise Recovery Cafe exist to plant hope where it is needed most. We connect people in recovery with community, practical resources, and a place to belong—because recovery grows best when no one does it alone.

If you or someone you love is in crisis

Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (24/7, free, confidential).
SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357 (24/7 treatment referral, English & Spanish).
Life-threatening emergency: call 911.
